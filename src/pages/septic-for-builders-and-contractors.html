When does a construction job usually need the tank opened?

Common moments: before a remodel that will add bedrooms or a lot of new plumbing, when a slab, driveway or pool is going in near the field, when the lid has disappeared under fill, or when a buyer or inspector wants the system looked at before a certificate or a closing. Rapid does not issue permits and does not publish a construction timeline. Drain field work in Florida may require a permit through the county health department; we'll talk through what we see after an inspection.

What should a crew avoid on the drain field while the job is open?

Don't park equipment, stockpile soil, or pour a pad on the field. Don't plant a row of trees along the lines as a "screen." Keep roof runoff and dewatering from pooling on the same soil that is supposed to take wastewater. If the field already looks wet, or if a temporary dumpster or a roll-off is sitting on a soft strip of yard, move it and call us before someone assumes the field has failed. A full tank and a compacted yard are different problems. We inspect first and repair when that's still possible.

The previous builder buried the lid. Can you still reach the tank?

Yes. Locating a buried lid is routine on older Lehigh Acres lots and on jobs where the grade has changed. That extra work can change the total on a pump-out, and we confirm it before we start. If you want the next trade, or the next pump-out, to skip the shovel, ask about a riser so the lid sits at ground level. We do that work; there is no riser price published here, so it is quoted on the call.

How do you price a jobsite pump-out?

A standard residential pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, $449.99 for 4 bedrooms and $499.99 for 5 bedrooms. A house under construction or a tank that is hard to reach is not automatically that number; tell us the address, what is in the way, and whether the tank is for a finished house or a job in progress, and we'll confirm a price before we come out. Commercial or unusual tanks are quoted. We are open 24 hours if a backup shows up after a crew has been using a temporary hookup or a house that is already occupied during a remodel.

What should I put in the handoff to the owner or the next trade?

Leave the lid visible or marked, keep a copy of the pump-out or inspection, and note anything we told you about the field. Do you pull drain field permits for a new field?

Drain field work in Florida may require a permit through the county health department. We inspect first and discuss what the lot needs. We do not publish a permit package or a replacement price on this page.

Can you set a riser before we close up the yard?

Yes. A riser brings the lid up to ground level so later pump-outs don't start with digging. Ask when you schedule the visit; the riser itself is quoted, not listed as a published price.

Is the $399.99 price for a house that is still under construction?

That figure is the standard pump-out for a 1–3 bedroom home. A tank that's hard to reach, unfinished access, or a non-standard tank is confirmed on the call, not assumed from the residential list.
