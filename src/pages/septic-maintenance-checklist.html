What belongs on the calendar, not on a wish list?

The last pump-out date, and a reminder in the EPA's three-to-five-year window unless Rapid told you to come back sooner. A larger household, a smaller tank, or a garbage disposal can shorten that window. If you just bought a Lehigh Acres house and the file is empty, treat the tank as due now and use the first-time owner guide for the rest of the setup. A standard pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, $449.99 for 4 bedrooms and $499.99 for 5 bedrooms, confirmed before we start. Once the lid is open, a routine visit is often about an hour; locating a buried lid adds time and can change the total.

What should never go down a Lehigh Acres tank?

Wipes of any kind, including those labeled flushable; cooking grease, fat and oil; paper towels and feminine products; paint, solvents and medications; and large amounts of harsh cleaner. A septic-safe roll of toilet paper is a better everyday choice than a thick wipe. Additives are not a substitute for pumping; solids still have to be taken out with a truck. Hang this list in a rental bathroom if you are a landlord.

What should I watch in the yard?

Don't park, pave, or stack a shed on the drain field. Grass is better cover than trees or heavy shrubs. After a long rain, a soft yard is common here; a spot that stays wet, smells, or grows a much greener strip is a reason to call, and the details are on when the drain field isn't draining. If you know where the lids are, mark them. If you don't, we can locate a buried lid when we come to pump.

How should I use water so the tank can do its job?

Don't run every load of laundry on the same morning. Fix a toilet that runs. A sudden flood of water can push solids toward the field before they have time to settle. In the rainy season, or after a storm, cut back until the yard has dried. If a hurricane is on the map, look at the prep page before the roads close.

When does the checklist turn into a phone call?

Odor in the house or the yard, gurgling, a drain that used to be fine and now isn't, sewage on the floor, or an alarm if your system has one. For water coming back up, stop using water and follow the backup steps. Rapid is open 24 hours at {{PHONE}}.

FAQ

Checklist questions

How often should I pump if I follow this list?

The EPA's general guidance for most household tanks is every three to five years. Habits help, but they don't replace a pump-out. If you have no date, call (239) 506-1163 and schedule one.

Do additives let me skip a pump-out?

No. Rapid's pumping pages already note that additives are not a substitute for pumping. Solids still build up and still have to be removed with a truck.

What does a routine pump-out cost if I'm just staying current?

A standard pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, $449.99 for 4 bedrooms and $499.99 for 5 bedrooms. We confirm your price before we start.

Can I give this checklist to a tenant or a guest?

Yes. The "what not to flush" and "stay off the field" lines are the parts most people need. Landlords and property managers can also share this page with occupants.
