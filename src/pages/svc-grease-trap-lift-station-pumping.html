About grease trap & lift station pumping

Grease trap pumping

A grease trap catches fats, oils and grease before they reach the sewer or septic system. Once it fills, grease passes through and causes clogs and backups. Pumping removes the grease and solids so the trap works again. Your local utility or county may set how often a commercial grease trap has to be pumped, so check its requirements.

Lift station pumping

Where wastewater can't flow downhill to a tank or sewer, a lift station collects it in a wet well and pumps it on. Grease, debris and sludge build up in the wet well over time. Pumping it out keeps the pumps and floats working properly.
