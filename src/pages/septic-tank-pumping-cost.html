What can change the price

  • Tank size. Bigger homes usually have bigger tanks.
  • Finding and reaching the lid. A buried lid has to be located and dug up first.
  • Time since the last pump-out. A tank left for many years can need more work.
  • Extra work. Repairs, emergency backups or other services are quoted separately.

FAQ

Septic tank pumping cost FAQ

How much does septic tank pumping cost?

A standard pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, $449.99 for a 4 bedroom home and $499.99 for a 5 bedroom home. Locating a buried lid or extra work can change the total; we'll tell you before we start.

How often should a septic tank be pumped in Florida?

Most homes need it every 3–5 years, depending on tank size and how many people live there. See septic tank pumping for more.
