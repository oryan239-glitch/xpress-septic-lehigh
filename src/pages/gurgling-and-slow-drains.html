When is it only one fixture, and when is it the tank?

A single bathroom sink that has been slow for months, with every other drain fine, can be a local clog. When the toilet, the shower and a tub on another wall all start to gurgle after laundry day, the tank or the line out to it is a better suspect. A full tank, a blocked outlet or filter, or a field that isn't taking water can all show up the same way from inside the house. You won't tell those apart with a plunger. Call {{PHONE}} and describe which fixtures are involved and how long it has been going on.

What should I do while I wait for a visit?

Cut back laundry and long showers. Don't keep "testing" the toilet. Skip chemical drain openers; they can set back the bacteria in the tank and do not empty sludge. If water starts coming up, stop all use and go to the backup steps. If you also notice a sewage odor or a soft strip in the yard, mention that on the call; those pages are sewage smell and wet spots.

What does Rapid do for a slow-drain call?

Most of the time the useful first step is to open the tank and pump it if it is due or full, then look at the inlet and the outlet once it's empty. What makes gurgling more common in a busy Lehigh Acres house?

A tank that is overdue, a garbage disposal that adds solids, a lot of laundry on one day, or wipes that should have gone in the trash. The checklist and the drain field do's and don'ts cover those habits. They will not clear a tank that already needs a truck. If you have no last pump-out date, treat the system as due and schedule the visit rather than waiting for a floor full of water.

FAQ

Gurgling and slow-drain questions

Should I use a chemical drain opener first?

No. It will not empty the tank and a heavy dose of harsh cleaner can set back the bacteria the system needs. Call (239) 506-1163 and describe the fixtures that are slow.

Is a gurgle after a load of laundry an emergency?

Not always. Ease off the water and call us to schedule a look. If water is standing in a shower or a toilet won't go down, treat it as a backup and call right away. We are open 24 hours.

Could this just be a clogged outlet filter?

It could be, if your tank has a filter. It could also be a full tank or a field problem. Ask us to look at the outlet when we open the tank. We don't publish a separate filter-cleaning price.
