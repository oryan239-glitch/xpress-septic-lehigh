Septic in Cape Coral

Why septic tank pumping matters in Cape Coral

Cape Coral, in Lee County, is known for its network of hundreds of miles of canals. The city has spent years extending central water and sewer through its Utilities Extension Program, but many neighborhoods, particularly in the north and northwest of the city, are still on septic tanks.

With so many homes close to canals and a high water table, a healthy septic system matters for the waterways as well as the house. Pumping on schedule keeps solids out of the drain field so it can do its job.

Septic tank pumping in Cape Coral: FAQ

My Cape Coral street is scheduled for sewer. Should I still pump my septic tank?

Until your home is actually connected, your septic system still has to work. If the tank is due, pumping it keeps the house running until the switch-over. The city may also have its own rules for abandoning the tank once you connect, so check with Cape Coral Utilities.
