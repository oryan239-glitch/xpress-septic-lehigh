Septic in Bonita Springs

Why septic tank pumping matters in Bonita Springs

Bonita Springs is in southern Lee County between Estero and Naples, along the Imperial River. Much of the city is on central sewer, but some older neighborhoods and properties toward the eastern, more rural side still use septic systems.

Like the rest of the coast, Bonita Springs has low, flat land and a water table that rises in the rainy season, which is hard on drain fields. Regular septic tank pumping keeps solids from clogging them.
