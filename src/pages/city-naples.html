Septic in Naples

Why septic tank pumping matters in Naples

Naples is on the Gulf coast in Collier County. The city itself is largely on central sewer, but large parts of the surrounding unincorporated area, including much of Golden Gate Estates and other rural-residential neighborhoods east of the city, rely on private wells and septic systems.

Low elevation and a high water table, especially during the summer wet season, leave less room for a drain field to treat wastewater. A tank that's pumped on schedule keeps sludge from reaching that drain field.
