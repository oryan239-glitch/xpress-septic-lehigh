Is a change of tenant a good time to empty the tank?

Often, yes, especially if you cannot prove the last service date, if the last household was large, or if the incoming lease will start with a full house. You get access without someone else's furniture over a lid, and you can mark the lid before new tenants move in. A standard pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, $449.99 for 4 bedrooms and $499.99 for 5 bedrooms. We confirm the price before we start. If the lid is buried, locating and digging can change the total.

What should I tell tenants on day one?

Keep it to a few lines they will actually read: no wipes (even the ones marked flushable), no grease down the kitchen sink, no parking on the drain field, and call you right away if a drain is slow or the yard smells. Point longer-term occupants to the maintenance checklist or the first-time owner guide if they have only lived on city sewer. You do not need a long lease clause to cover every product brand; the habits above prevent most avoidable backups.

Who should call if the rental backs up after hours?

Give tenants a number that answers. Rapid is open 24 hours, 7 days a week, at {{PHONE}}. They should stop running water, including the washing machine, and not keep flushing "to see if it cleared." The walkthrough is on what to do during a backup. Emergency work is quoted on the call; this site does not list a separate after-hours price. If you use a property manager for the same houses, share this page and the property manager page so both of you aren't improvising at midnight.

What records should I keep for the next lease or a sale?

The pump-out date, what we told you about the tank, and where the lid is. If you later sell the rental, that file is exactly what a buyer or an agent will ask for; see buying or selling a home with a tank. If a tenant reports a wet strip in the yard after a week of rain, don't assume the field has failed. Saturated soil in Lehigh Acres is common in the rainy season. We can look at it, and we repair a field when that's still possible rather than jumping to replacement.

Landlord questions about Lehigh Acres tanks

Can I schedule a pump-out between leases?

Yes. Call (239) 506-1163 and tell us the house will be vacant. A standard pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, confirmed before we start.

Will you deal directly with my tenant if there is a backup?

We can come out when someone with authority calls and can provide access. Many landlords have the tenant call us and then confirm the visit. Rapid is open 24 hours.

How often should a rental tank be pumped?

The EPA's general range for most household tanks is every three to five years. A rental that stays full, or turns over often, may need service sooner. If you have no date, schedule a pump-out rather than guessing.

The last tenants left grease and wipes in the system. Is that a repair?

Start with a pump-out and a look at the inlet and outlet. Wipes and grease are a common reason a tank or a filter backs up. Further repair, if needed, is quoted after we see the system.
