What should I do while the yard is underwater?

Use as little water as you can. Don't open the tank yourself. Don't ask for a pump-out in standing water. The U.S. EPA's flood guidance is to wait until the soil is no longer waterlogged; a tank pumped in those conditions can shift or float. If wastewater comes up inside the house, keep people and pets away from it, stop adding water, and call {{PHONE}} so we can tell you whether we can enter the lot yet. Why does the rainy season in Lehigh Acres make tanks look worse?

Much of this area has sandy soil and a high water table. In a wet month there is less room between the drain field and groundwater, so a tank that is already full, or a field that is already tired, shows itself sooner. That is also why a soggy yard in August is not, by itself, proof the field has failed. Cut back on water, then call if the house is affected or the wet spot stays after the rest of the yard has dried. Everyday habits that help in any month are on the maintenance checklist.
