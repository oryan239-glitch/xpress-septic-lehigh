Septic in Fort Myers

Why septic tank pumping matters in Fort Myers

Fort Myers is the county seat of Lee County and sits on the south bank of the Caloosahatchee River. Central sewer covers much of the city, but many homes in older neighborhoods and in the unincorporated areas around the city still rely on a septic tank and drain field.

Southwest Florida's flat land, sandy soil and seasonally high water table mean drain fields here work hard, especially in the summer rainy season. Keeping solids out of the drain field with regular septic tank pumping is the cheapest way to protect it.
