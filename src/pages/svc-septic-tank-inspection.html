What's involved

About septic tank inspection

During an inspection the tank is opened so its condition can be seen: sludge and scum levels, the inlet and outlet, baffles, lids and any signs of cracks or water coming back from the drain field. Inspections are often done together with a pump-out, because an empty tank is the easiest to check.

When to get a septic tank inspection

  • Before buying or selling a home with septic
  • Slow drains, gurgling or odors
  • Wet or unusually green spots over the drain field
  • You don't know when the system was last serviced

Florida has state rules for septic systems; the Florida DEP Onsite Sewage Program explains them. Ask us when you call what your situation requires.

FAQ

Septic Tank Inspection FAQ

Should the tank be pumped during an inspection?

Often, yes. With the tank empty it's much easier to see the walls, baffles and inlet and outlet.
