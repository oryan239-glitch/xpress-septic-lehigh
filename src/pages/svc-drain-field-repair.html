What's involved

About drain field repair

In Southwest Florida, sandy soil and a water table that rises in the summer rainy season can leave a drain field with little room to work. When solids escape a tank that hasn't been pumped, they clog the field even faster.

Signs of drain field problems

  • Standing water or soggy ground over the field
  • Grass that's much greener over the drain lines
  • Sewage odor outdoors
  • Drains that slow down or back up, especially after rain

The first step is usually pumping the tank and checking it, so we can tell whether the problem is the tank, the lines or the field itself. Then we'll explain the repair options. Drain field work in Florida may require a permit through the county health department.

FAQ

Drain Field Repair FAQ

Will pumping fix a failing drain field?

Pumping alone doesn't fix a clogged field, but it takes the load off and stops more solids getting in. It's also the first step in finding out what's wrong.

Why does my drain field act up after heavy rain?

When the ground is saturated, there's less room for wastewater to soak in, so a weak drain field shows symptoms first after big storms.
