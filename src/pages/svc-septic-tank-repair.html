What's involved

About septic tank repair

Common septic tank repairs include replacing cracked or missing lids, fixing broken inlet or outlet baffles, clearing or repairing the line from the house to the tank, and installing risers so the lid sits at ground level for future pump-outs.

Signs your septic tank may need repair

  • Sewage odors in the yard or house
  • Drains backing up even after a pump-out
  • A lid that's cracked, sunken or unsafe
  • Water flowing back into the tank from the drain field

Repairs usually start with pumping the tank so the problem can be seen. If the problem is in the drain field, see drain field repair.

FAQ

Septic Tank Repair FAQ

Do you need to pump the tank before repairing it?

Usually. Most repairs inside or at the tank need it empty so the problem can be seen and fixed safely.

Can you add a riser to my septic tank?

Yes. A riser brings the lid up to ground level so you don't have to dig every time. Ask when you call {{PHONE}}.
