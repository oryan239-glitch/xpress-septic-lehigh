What can I check before anyone brings a shovel?

Walk the yard for a slight mound, a square or round outline in the grass, a cleanout, or a spot a previous owner marked with a painted cap or a paver. Look at any old permit, as-built, or pump-out receipt in the closing file. Neighbors sometimes remember where a truck parked last time, but that is only a hint. If you find a candidate, mark it and stop. Don't pry at a lid you can't lift safely, and don't park on a patch that might be the drain field. The step-by-step of how we search is on septic tank locating in Lehigh Acres; this page is for the homeowner who has already walked the lot and come up empty.

Why do so many lids disappear on this grid?

Sod grows over them. Fill gets spread after a fence or a pool goes in. Some lots have more than one access — an inlet lid and an outlet lid — and only one was ever left visible. Sandy soil and a rainy season can settle dirt over a lid that was flush with the ground last year. None of that means the tank has moved. It means the next visit should start with a search, not with a guess.

Why shouldn't I just dig until I hit something?

Because irrigation lines, electrical and gas lines can be in the same area. Rapid already tells homeowners to call 811 before any digging of their own. A missed pipe is a much worse afternoon than a scheduled locate. If the house also has a pool or an irrigation layout near the tank, tell us that when you book so we know the yard is tight.

What if you only find one lid and the tank has two?

Say you want both accesses opened if they exist. Our inlet-and-outlet page explains why that matters. We empty sludge and scum, not just the liquid, and we check the inlet and the outlet once the tank is empty. A missing second lid is exactly the kind of thing a locate is for.

Can I have a riser put on once you find it?

Yes. A riser brings the lid up to ground level so later visits don't start with digging. Ask when you schedule; the riser is quoted, not listed as a published price.

The lot was regraded last year. Can you still find the tank?

Often yes, though fill makes the search harder. Mention the regrade, any pool or irrigation work, and whatever records you have so we can plan the visit.
