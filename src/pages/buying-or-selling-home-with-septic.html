If I am the buyer, what should I ask for before I waive a septic review?

Ask for the last pump-out receipt, any prior inspection, and whether the house is truly on a tank. Part of Lehigh Acres is on FGUA sewer; a sewer charge on the bill is a clue the system may already be a utility connection. If the house is on a tank and the file is empty, budget for a visit. Rapid's inspection is built for this kind of sale. With the tank empty, we can see sludge and scum levels, the inlet and outlet, baffles, lids, and whether water is coming back from the field. We do not publish a separate inspection price; we'll confirm it when you call. This visit is not a whole-house inspection and it does not decide a loan.

If I am the seller, when should I have the tank emptied?

Earlier is calmer than the week of closing. A recent pump-out and a clear lid give buyers fewer last-minute surprises. If you cannot find the lid, say so up front; locating a buried lid is ordinary work here, and it can change the total. A standard pump-out is $399.99 for a 1–3 bedroom home, $449.99 for 4 bedrooms and $499.99 for 5 bedrooms, confirmed before we start. Repairs, if something is broken, are quoted after we see the system. We install risers when you want the lid at ground level for later visits; there is no riser price on this site, so ask on the call.

What does the visit look like on a sale-day calendar?

We need access to the yard and a place to stand the truck. Pets inside, gates unlocked, and a lockbox or a person on site all help. Once the lid is open, a routine pump-out is often about an hour; locating and digging add time. If the contract has a hard date, say that when you book. We do not promise a closing date on this page. After the visit we tell you what we saw. If the field looks like it is not taking water, that conversation may move to drain field repair or, only if a repair will not hold, replacement.

What should a first-time septic buyer do after the keys are in hand?

Keep the service date, don't park on the field, and read the guide for new owners in Lehigh Acres. If you are working with an agent, they may already have that page. Your checklist is the short version for the fridge. If drains back up the first weekend, stop using water and call; we are open 24 hours and the steps are on the backup page.

FAQ

Buying and selling FAQ for Lehigh Acres tanks

Should the tank be pumped as part of a sale inspection?

Often, yes. With the tank empty it's much easier to see the walls, baffles and inlet and outlet. Tell us it's for a sale when you call (239) 506-1163.

Do you set the price of a pump-out now, or only after you arrive?

The published standard pump-out is $399.99 for 1–3 bedrooms, $449.99 for 4 bedrooms and $499.99 for 5 bedrooms. We confirm your price before we start. A buried lid or other extra work can change the total.

Will this inspection satisfy a lender on its own?

Rapid does not decide whether a loan is approved and does not replace the general home inspection. Ask your lender what they need, then tell us so the visit matches the request as far as we can.

The closing is next week and nobody can find the lid. Can you still help?

Call (239) 506-1163 and say you have a closing date. We locate buried lids, but we do not guarantee a date on this page. The sooner you call, the better the odds of getting on the schedule.
